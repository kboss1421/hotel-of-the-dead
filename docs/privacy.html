Privacy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

What stays on your device

Your progress, levels, prestige, BP, unlocks, classes, cosmetics and settings are saved only in your browser's storage (or the desktop app's storage) on your own device. Clearing the site's data in your browser (or the desktop app's data folder) deletes them.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

The game has no accounts, no email sign-up, no advertising, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no real-money purchases. BP is an in-game currency only.

Online play

While the game is open it stays connected to the online hub (hotd-hub.onrender.com), so it can show who is online and receive invites. It sends a random player ID made by the game, the name you choose, your skin and banner, your friend code, your party and match choices, and, during a match, what your character does (position, shots, hits and so on). Other players in your party or match see your name, banner and character. The hub keeps this in memory only while you are connected, forgets players who have been gone for about two minutes, and does not write it to disk.

Voice chat

Voice is only sent while you hold the talk key, and only after you allow microphone access. It goes directly between the players in your match (peer to peer, WebRTC) and is not recorded or stored. To connect players, the browser asks Google's public STUN servers for your public network address.

Servers run by players

If you join a server that another player runs (Start Server.bat or HOST in the desktop app), that person's computer receives the same match data and can see your IP address.

Hosting providers

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and the hub on Render. Like any website host, they may keep standard access logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13. Names you choose and voice chat are visible or audible to other players, so do not use your real name.
