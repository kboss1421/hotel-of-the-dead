    CONTROLS

    • WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump
    • Walk into buildings through their doors; jump through windows and over low fences
    • Ctrl tap to crouch, hold while running to slide (C works too)
    • Mouse aim, LMB fire, RMB aim down sights
    • R reload, 1/2/Wheel swap guns
    • F buy / use, V knife, hold Q / E to lean left / right
    • G lethal (press again to set off C4), Z tactical, X shock blast (zombies)
    • 3 4 5 scorestreaks, Tab scoreboard (multiplayer)
    • B emote wheel (then 1-4)
    • T hold to talk in online matches (change it in Settings)
    • Controller (Xbox / PlayStation): left stick move, click it to sprint, right stick look, RT fire, LT aim, A jump, B crouch / slide, X reload (hold to use, buy, plant), Y swap, RB lethal, LB tactical, click right stick to knife, D-pad scorestreaks, View scoreboard, Menu pause. In menus: D-pad moves, A selects, B back
    • Esc menu: Return, Settings, Leave game (or Close game on the main menu)
